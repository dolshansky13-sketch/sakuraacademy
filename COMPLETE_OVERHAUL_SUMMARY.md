# Sakura Academy - Complete Overhaul Summary

## 🎮 New Conversation System

### Branching Dialogue Engine
- **Fully interactive conversations** with multiple choice paths
- **Context-aware triggers** based on:
  - Location (library, track, gym, etc.)
  - Weather (rainy, sunny, snowy, cloudy)
  - Time of day (morning, afternoon, evening, night)
  - Relationship stage (stranger → partner)
  - Affection level (0-100)
  - Tension level (0-100)
  - Player stats (academics, athletics, charm, creativity)
  - Inventory items
  - Previous conversation history

### Conversation Features
- **Multiple outcomes** based on player choices
- **Stat requirements** for certain dialogue options (e.g., Charm 20+ for flirting)
- **Affection/tension effects** from choices
- **Mood tracking** - characters remember their emotional state
- **Flag system** - track story progress and unlock new content
- **Conditional choices** - some options only appear if conditions are met

### Example Conversation Flow
```
1. Meet Sakura in library (rainy day, affection 30+)
2. Choose dialogue option:
   - Friendly → +3 affection
   - Direct → +5 affection
   - Flirt (requires Charm 20+) → +7 affection, +5 tension
3. Each choice leads to different conversation paths
4. Outcomes affect relationship progression
```

## 🎯 Gameplay Systems

### Stat Training
- **Academics** - Study at apartment (+5 per session)
- **Athletics** - Exercise at apartment (+5 per session)
- **Charm** - Socialize at apartment (+5 per session)
- **Creativity** - Create at apartment (+5 per session)

Stats affect:
- Conversation options (stat requirements)
- Flirt success rates
- Character reactions
- Secret scene unlocks

### Relationship Stages
1. **Stranger** (0-9 affection) - First meetings
2. **Acquaintance** (10-29) - Getting to know each other
3. **Friend** (30-54) - Comfortable friendship
4. **Close Friend** (55-74) - Deep bond forming
5. **Romance** (75-94) - Romantic relationship
6. **Partner** (95-100) - Committed relationship

Each stage unlocks:
- New dialogue options
- Conversation trees
- Secret scenes
- Intimate interactions
- Special choices (hold hands, kiss, etc.)

### Tension System
- Tracks romantic/physical tension (0-100)
- Increased by:
  - Flirting (especially bold moves)
  - Intimate gifts (lingerie, special items)
  - Bold dialogue choices
  - Physical contact (hold hands, kiss)
- Required for:
  - Secret intimate scenes
  - Advanced relationship options
  - Outfit try-on scenes

### Gift System
**Regular Gifts** (Café):
- Food items, books, accessories
- Affection bonuses: 1-12 points
- Character preferences affect bonus

**Special Gifts** (Café):
- Sakura Ring, Champion Trophy, Golden Microphone
- Moonlight Necklace, Eternal Rose, Promise Ring
- Affection bonuses: 14-25 points
- Higher tension increases

**Outfits** (Fashion Boutique):
- 12 cosplay/costume outfits
- Affection bonuses: 10-18 points
- Tension bonuses included
- Character preferences

### Secret Scenes
**10 Secret Scenes** (2 per character):
- Triggered by specific conditions
- Intimate/romantic content

- Unique dialogue

**Example Requirements:**

- Stat requirements (Athletics 60+ for Yuki)
